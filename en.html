Controls

  • Moving an element: grab a pawn, marker or card and drop it in a new place.
  • Views: the tabs at the top switch between the main board, the market with the decks and the player boards. To move a pawn or card to another view, drop it on a tab (e.g. a market card on a player's tab).
  • Moving the view: drag an empty spot of the table. Zoom: mouse wheel or the + and − buttons.
  • Card preview: hover over a face-up card; an enlarged view appears in the bottom left corner.
  • Double click: on a card it flips it, on a worker it lays it down or stands it up.
  • Right mouse button: element menu, e.g. “Take into hand”, “Put on top of the deck”, “Shuffle”.
  • New round: the button on the top bar moves the round marker, lays out 12 market cards from the current era deck, reveals the event and colonization cards (and the barbarians if the event has their symbol), returns the ambassadors and passes the first player marker. Players don't touch these decks.
  • Colonization: open bidding starting from the first player. Barbarians: a secret declaration by each player; the result is shown once everyone has declared.
  • Production: the production level is read from the marker position on the track. Drag the marker or use ▲▼ in the player panel. The “Production” button on the top bar calculates the production phase: income, food and army upkeep paid from resources, culture and Imperial cards.
  • Decks: drag from a deck to the table to reveal a card, or onto the hand bar to draw it into your hand. A card dropped on a deck of the same kind goes on top.
  • Hand: only you can see the cards on the bottom bar. Drag a card to the table to play it.
  • Undo: reverts the last move in the log, no matter who made it.
