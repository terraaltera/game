TERRA ALTERA

A Song of the Forgotten Kingdoms

About

In an alternative world, where history took a different course, new kingdoms vie for supremacy over the fate of humankind. The ageing Emperor is seeking a successor. If you wish to become one, you must lead your civilisation to prosperity and cultural dominance, making wise use of your ambassadors and the potential of your dominion.

In Terra Altera you guide your people by developing technologies, discovering new territories, managing resources and population, raising wonders and using the special abilities of your leader. You are not alone, however — rival civilisations will play to their own strengths and strike at your weaknesses.

Terra Altera combines classic and innovative elements of a civilisation game: population and resources, technologies and wonders, leaders and diplomacy, aggression and wars, caravans and barbarians, events, as well as forts and a strategy track. Together they create a rich universe and offer many paths of development.

The game is played on the main board, known as the Imperial Court, and on individual player boards, and is designed for 2–4 players. Whichever path you choose, your goal remains the same: cultural dominance over the other kingdoms. You have the chance to become the new Emperor. Remember — the future of Terra Altera is in your hands.

Mechanics

The core mechanic of the game is placing action pawns on the main board and playing imperial cards. This triggers actions that develop your civilisation, visible both on your player board and on the main board.

Actions are triggered by sending pawns to the main board. There are three types of action pawns — Ambassador, Tribe and Pyramid — as well as workers.

Players take turns placing pawns on the corresponding action spaces of the main board. Workers are sent to technologies and territories to produce resources and strength.

  • Tribesingle-use action pawn 1–7

Actions & Resources

Actions

During your turn you choose one of the main actions, e.g. buying a technology, increasing your population, discovering a territory, or sending a worker to technologies or territories to produce resources or increase your strength.

Resources

Your civilisation relies on resources that drive its growth: energy, stone, gold, influence, culture, as well as strength and valour. Imperial cards are also a resource in the game. Each resource is needed to develop a different branch of your civilisation; for example, gold lets you discover territories and buy technology and wonder cards, while energy lets your population grow.

Main actions

  • Buy a technology
  • Increase population
  • Discover a territory
  • Send a worker to a territory
  • Send a worker to a civil technology
  • Send a warrior to a military technology or build a fort
  • Buy a wonder (gold) or build a wonder stage (stone)
  • Any action – one of the above
